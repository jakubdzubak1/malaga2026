🌉 Puente Nuevo + El Tajo Gorge
The 120m-deep gorge spanned by the 1793 stone bridge is Ronda's iconic landmark. Walk both sides of the gorge. Best view: descend to Jardines de Cuenca below the bridge via the path from Plaza María Auxiliadora — far more dramatic than looking from the top. The gorge itself is ideal for photography and short walks along the cliff edges.
🆓 Bridge + gorge: free Museum inside bridge: €2 ⏱️ Allow 45–60 min
16:00
🏛️ Palacio de Mondragón
Former residence of the last Moorish king of Ronda, later received the Catholic Monarchs. Three courtyards in different architectural styles — Moorish mosaics, an impressive Mudéjar ceiling in the Noble Hall, and a museum tracing the region's history from cave dwellers to the Reconquista. The gorge-edge garden is a highlight with spectacular views over El Tajo. One of the best-value sights in Ronda.
🎟️ ~€4/person · free for EU citizens ⏰ Tue–Fri 9:30–19:00 · Sat 10:00–18:00 · Sun 10:00–15:00 🆓 Free on Wednesdays ⏱️ Allow 60–90 min
17:30
🐂 Plaza de Toros
Spain's oldest and most prestigious bullring, built in 1785 — the birthplace of modern bullfighting as codified by Pedro Romero. The elegant neoclassical arena seats 5,000. The museum inside covers the history of the sport and the Romero dynasty. Views from inside the ring over the gorge are worth the entry even if bullfighting isn't your interest.
🎟️ €8/person · €16 for 2 ⏰ Open until ~19:00
19:00
🕌 Arab Baths + La Ciudad stroll
Walk down to Baños Árabes — among the best-preserved Arab baths in Spain, tucked below the old town near the river. Then wander back through La Ciudad, the original Moorish medina: Minaret de San Sebastián, whitewashed lanes, and local squares. Time it for the golden-hour light.
Arab baths: €3 La Ciudad streets: free

🔵 Júzcar — The Smurf Village

Continue on the A-397 toward Marbella. The best viewpoint on this entire route — halfway along the mountain pass at ~700m altitude. Pull over and enjoy sweeping views of the Genal Valley and the Mediterranean coast below. On clear days you can see Gibraltar. Also look for the Rainer Maria Rilke monument at km 16 — a tribute to the poet who called Ronda the "Dream City".

🪨 El Torcal de Antequera
A surreal Jurassic limestone landscape sculpted over 150 million years. Towers, labyrinths, and geological formations that look like another planet. Three hiking routes: Ruta Verde (easy, 45 min loop), Ruta Amarilla (moderate, 2.5h), Ruta Naranja (harder). Start early before 10:00 — no shade in July. Bring 1.5L water per person. Park at the Interpretation Centre (free parking). On weekends, arrive early or the shuttle bus operates from roadside parking.

Getting Around

Public Transport in Málaga

Use for all city and evening trips (7–10 July before car, and 11–13 July after returning it).

🎫
Get the Bonobus Card
10 trips for €8.40 (vs €1.40 single). Works on all EMT buses. Shareable between 2 people. Buy at kiosks, estancos (tobacco shops), or EMT stops. Free 60-min transfer between buses.
🚂
Cercanías C1 for Airport
Train from AGP Airport to Centro-Alameda: 12 min, €1.80. Runs every 20–30 min from early morning to midnight. Tap contactless card at barrier — no ticket machine needed.
🚌
Key Bus Lines
Line 35 → hotel area (Cruz de Humilladero). Line 16 → near Gibralfaro (short walk). Lines 3 + 11 → La Malagueta beach. Google Maps works well for Málaga bus routes.
🗺️
The City is Walkable
Alcazaba, Picasso Museum, Cathedral, Muelle Uno, and Calle Larios are all within 15 min walk of each other. Evening visits: walk from the centre, bus back if tired.
📱
EMT Málaga App
Real-time bus arrivals at every stop, journey planner, favourite stops. Also fully integrated in Google Maps. Download before arriving.
🧍
Queue Culture
Málaga locals take bus queuing seriously. Always ask "¿quién es el último?" (who is last?). Front door entry only. Drivers don't give change for notes larger than €5.

🔍
Where to compare
DiscoverCars.com shows all-in prices with insurance included. MalagaCar.com is the local specialist. Compare both — same car can differ by €30–50 total.
🚗
Manual vs automatic
Almost all cheap cars in Spain are manual. Automatics cost 30–50% more and sell out in July. Book automatic explicitly and early if needed.
💳
Credit card deposit
A credit card (not debit) is required — typically €500–1,500 blocked. Released within 5–10 days of return. Exception: Marbesol requires no deposit at all.
📸
Photo every panel at pickup
Before driving away, photograph every panel, wheel arch, windscreen, and interior — timestamped. Walk with the agent. This is your protection against false damage claims at drop-off.
⛽
Fuel policy
Always choose "full-to-full" — pick up full, return full. Avoid "full-to-empty" where you prepay fuel at inflated rates. Fill the tank yourself at a petrol station near the airport before return.
