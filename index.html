🪨 El Torcal de Antequera
A surreal Jurassic limestone landscape sculpted over 150 million years. Towers, labyrinths, and geological formations that look like another planet. Three hiking routes: Ruta Verde (easy, 45 min loop), Ruta Amarilla (moderate, 2.5h), Ruta Naranja (harder). Start early before 10:00 — no shade in July. Bring 1.5L water per person. Park at the Interpretation Centre (free parking). On weekends, arrive early or the shuttle bus operates from roadside parking.

Getting Around

Public Transport in Málaga

Use for all city and evening trips (7–10 July before car, and 11–13 July after returning it).

🎫
Get the Bonobus Card
10 trips for €8.40 (vs €1.40 single). Works on all EMT buses. Shareable between 2 people. Buy at kiosks, estancos (tobacco shops), or EMT stops. Free 60-min transfer between buses.
🚂
Cercanías C1 for Airport
Train from AGP Airport to Centro-Alameda: 12 min, €1.80. Runs every 20–30 min from early morning to midnight. Tap contactless card at barrier — no ticket machine needed.
🚌
Key Bus Lines
Line 35 → hotel area (Cruz de Humilladero). Line 16 → near Gibralfaro (short walk). Lines 3 + 11 → La Malagueta beach. Google Maps works well for Málaga bus routes.
🗺️
The City is Walkable
Alcazaba, Picasso Museum, Cathedral, Muelle Uno, and Calle Larios are all within 15 min walk of each other. Evening visits: walk from the centre, bus back if tired.
📱
EMT Málaga App
Real-time bus arrivals at every stop, journey planner, favourite stops. Also fully integrated in Google Maps. Download before arriving.
🧍
Queue Culture
Málaga locals take bus queuing seriously. Always ask "¿quién es el último?" (who is last?). Front door entry only. Drivers don't give change for notes larger than €5.

Car Rental

Rental Car — Málaga Airport

Pick up 9 July morning, return 13 July at AGP before your flight. ~4 days.

⚠️ Insurance is the #1 trap in Málaga car rentals Budget companies (Centauro, Goldcar) advertise €8/day but pressure you at the counter to buy their insurance, often doubling the total. Best defence: book via DiscoverCars or MalagaCar.com with full insurance already included in the price, or book with Enterprise/Alamo directly where pricing is transparent.
🥇 Enterprise — AGP Terminal
Best-rated at Málaga Airport (8.5/10). In-terminal desk, no shuttle. Transparent pricing, good fleet, no aggressive upselling.
In-terminal · 8.5/10~€35–50/day July
🥈 Alamo — AGP Terminal
Reliable, in-terminal, good value for 4-day rentals. Part of the Enterprise group so same quality standards.
In-terminal · 8.2/10~€30–45/day July
Centauro — Off-site shuttle
Cheap base price, but off-site (10-min shuttle). Pre-book their top insurance online to avoid counter pressure. Take photos of every scratch at pickup.
Shuttle required · 7.8/10~€15–25/day (excl. insurance)
🔍
Where to Book
Compare on MalagaCar.com (local specialist) or DiscoverCars.com (insurance included in shown price). Book 2–3 weeks ahead for best July rates.
🚗
Manual or Automatic?
Most cheap Spanish cars are manual. If you need automatic, book explicitly and early — they cost 30–50% more and sell out fast in summer.
💳
Credit Card Deposit
A credit card (not debit) blocks €500–1,500 as deposit. Bring a card with enough headroom. Released 5–10 days after return.
📸
Photo Every Panel
Before driving away, photograph every panel, wheel arch, and the interior with timestamped photos. Walk with the agent present. This protects against false damage claims at drop-off.
